A LETTER BEFORE WE BEGIN

Sis, you were chosen before you ever chose wrong.

Ye have not chosen me, but I have chosen you, and ordained you, that ye should go and bring forth fruit.JOHN 15:16, KJV

Most of us don’t end up in the same painful place twice because we’re foolish. We end up there because we’re tired, lonely, hopeful, or used to it, and in that moment we make the choice that feels familiar instead of the one that feels faithful.

This course is about the small choices. Who gets your first yes in the morning. What you do with the feeling that something is off. Whether you pause and pray or rush and regret. Big changes in your life are built out of a thousand small ones, and every one of them is a chance to choose God, and in choosing Him, to choose yourself.

I’m not writing this as someone who has it all figured out. I’m writing it as a woman still walking it out, one choice at a time, right beside you. We’re learning our worth together.

Growing with you,

Nik

MODULE 1

Start With God, Not the Feeling

Whoever gets your first yes in the morning shapes every yes after it.

UNDERSTANDYour first yes sets the tone

Before you say yes to anything else in your day — the text, the scroll, the worry, the to-do list — you’ve already made a choice about who leads. When God gets the first minutes, He gets a say in the rest. When the feeling gets them, it drives the whole day.

This isn’t about being perfect or praying for an hour. It’s about order. Seek first, and the rest finds its place.

PRACTICEWho gets my first yes?

Tap everything that usually gets your first ten minutes:

Starting tomorrow, my first five minutes belong to God. I’ll spend them before I touch . I’ll keep my Bible or journal .

REFLECT & PRAYReflect & Pray

But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you.MATTHEW 6:33, KJV

Prayer: Lord, take the first minutes of my day. Order my steps before the noise does. Amen.

AFFIRMATION

“God gets my first yes, and my whole day follows Him.”

MODULE 2

Know Your Pattern, Without the Shame

You can’t close a door you refuse to look at.

UNDERSTANDPatterns aren’t punishments

A pattern is just a path you’ve walked so many times your feet find it in the dark. It usually started as a way to survive: to feel wanted, to keep the peace, to not be alone. Seeing it clearly isn’t about blaming yourself. It’s about finally having a choice.

God’s mercy meets you right here. You don’t have to clean yourself up before you look.

MODULE 3

Your Worth Was Settled First

You don’t have to earn what God already declared.

UNDERSTANDIdentity before attention

When our worth is up for negotiation, we accept whatever is offered. When it’s settled, we can wait, discern and walk away. Your worth was decided before anyone texted back, before anyone chose you, before you got anything right.

MODULE 4

Discernment Before Attachment: The Fruit Check

Words can be beautiful. Fruit tells the truth.

UNDERSTANDLook at the fruit, not the feelings

Jesus didn’t say you’ll know people by their promises, their potential or how they make you feel at first. He said you’ll know them by their fruit: what consistently grows from their life over time. This works for anyone — a new friend, a job, a church, a situationship, and even you.

PRACTICERun a fruit check

Think of one person or situation. Rate how often you’ve actually seen each fruit — not hoped for it.

REFLECT & PRAYReflect & Pray

Ye shall know them by their fruits.MATTHEW 7:16, KJV

Prayer: Lord, give me eyes to see fruit, not just feelings. Help me trust what You show me. Amen.

AFFIRMATION

“I don’t have to guess. I can watch the fruit and trust what I see.”

MODULE 5

The Pause & Pray Method for Daily Choices

The space between the feeling and the yes is where God speaks.

UNDERSTANDMost regrets are rushed

Answering the late-night text. Saying yes to the plan. Going back “just to talk.” The choices we regret most were usually made fast, while we were tired, lonely or hurting. A pause isn’t weakness. It’s where wisdom gets a turn.

PRACTICETry it on a real decision

The next time I feel pulled to , I’ll wait , pray , and call before I decide.

REFLECT & PRAYReflect & Pray

Trust in the Lord with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.PROVERBS 3:5–6, KJV

Prayer: God, slow me down. Speak in the pause, and give me the courage to listen. Amen.

AFFIRMATION

“I don’t have to decide in a hurry. Peace can wait for my yes.”
