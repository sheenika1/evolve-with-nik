    The self-worth rebuild

    A faith-led workbook for the woman who gave until the cup ran dry, and is ready to fill it back up on purpose.

    Before we start

    Hey, sis.

    If you picked this up, I already know something about you. You've been the one everybody drinks from.

    The one who shows up. The one who remembers the birthday, covers the bill, holds the baby, takes the call at midnight. Somewhere in all that pouring, you stopped checking what was left for you.

    This isn't a book about becoming someone new. It's about coming back to the woman God already called worthy, before anyone ever had a chance to tell you otherwise.

    I'm not writing this from the finish line. I'm writing it from right beside you. We're learning our worth together, one honest page at a time.

    Thou anointest my head with oil; my cup runneth over.Psalm 23:5

    With love, Nik

    Meet Nik

    Growing right beside you

    I'm not your guru. I'm your big sister on the same road.

    I'm Nik, a mother of two boys from Florida, and a woman still learning her own worth out loud.

    For a long time I was the one everybody poured from. I didn't notice how empty I'd gotten until I had nothing left for me. Rebuilding has been slow, faith-led, and real, and Evolve With Nik is where I share the tools, prayers and honest lessons that are carrying me through.

    I also write music for women's empowerment under the artist name Alisha Inari, because some things heal better when you can sing them.

    Everything in this workbook is something I've had to practice myself. We're doing this together.

    How this works

    Go slow. Be honest. Come back.

    Refill moves through four parts. Each one builds on the last, so take them in order.

    Everything you type saves automatically on this device. Nobody sees your answers but you. Use the Contents button anytime to jump back to a page.

    Part one

    Empty

    You can't refill a cup you won't look into.

    Most of us don't run empty all at once. It's a slow leak. A yes we didn't mean. A text we answered at 1 a.m. A need we decided didn't matter because someone else's was louder.

    This part isn't about blame. It's about getting honest about where you are, so you know where to start.

    Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.Matthew 11:28

    Reflection

    Where the leak started

    Our patterns usually have a first page. Somewhere along the way you learned that being needed was the same as being loved.

    Go gently here. If a memory feels too heavy to sit with alone, it's okay to bring it to a counselor, your pastor, or a friend you trust.

    Part two

    Truth

    The stories you believe about yourself decide how much you'll let yourself receive.

    Some of those stories were handed to you by people who were hurting. Some you wrote yourself in your lowest seasons. Either way, they aren't the final word.

    In this part, you'll catch the old scripts and hold them up next to what God actually says about you.

    I will praise thee; for I am fearfully and wonderfully made: marvellous are thy works; and that my soul knoweth right well.Psalm 139:14

    Worksheet 3

    Old scripts I'm done reading

      Part three

      Rebuild

      Worth isn't something you earn back. It's something you stop giving away.

      Now that you've named the leaks and the lies, it's time to build something sturdy. That means knowing what you bring to the table, and deciding what you'll no longer allow at it.

      This is the part where you start protecting the refill before you even pour it.

      Worksheet 5

      My worth inventory

      Refill

      A full cup isn't a one-time fix. It's a rhythm.

      You don't need a spa weekend or a whole new life. You need small, steady pours that you protect like appointments, because they are.

      But they that wait upon the Lord shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles.Isaiah 40:31
